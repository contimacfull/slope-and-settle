Support

Need help with Slope and Settle? Write to OADsupport@gmail.com.

Before you write

  • The App needs no account and no internet connection to play.
  • Your progress lives only on your device. To start over use Settings > Reset progress.
  • Open-source licences: Settings > Open-source licenses.

What to include in a support request

  • Your device model and operating-system version (iOS or Android).
  • The App version shown in your store listing.
  • The level number and what you did before the problem.
