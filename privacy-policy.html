Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Applies to: Slope and Settle version 1.0.0 (pre-release)

This policy describes what the app Slope and Settle (the "App") does with information, based on how the App is built. The App is a single-player puzzle game that works without an internet connection.

1. Who is responsible

Developer / operator: Alexandru Dan. Contact: OADsupport@gmail.com. A postal address is not published; contact is by e-mail.

2. Information the developer collects

None. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no server operated by the developer, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking. The App contains no code that sends information to the developer or to third parties, and on Android it does not request the INTERNET permission.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress and preferences the App stores a small file of settings in its private storage on your device (Android SharedPreferences / iOS UserDefaults). It contains:

  • for each level you completed: the star rating and your fewest moves;
  • whether you finished the tutorial;
  • your choices for sound, haptics and reduced motion.

It contains no name, e-mail address, device identifier, location or other personal data. The developer cannot see it.

4. Permissions

The App requests no special permissions. Sound uses the system click sound; haptics use the device's vibration feedback, which needs no permission.

5. Backups, deleting and resetting data

  • You can erase your progress at any time in Settings > Reset progress.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its stored data from your device.
  • Device backups: your operating system may include the App's stored data in its own backups (for example Android Auto Backup, which is currently enabled for this App, or iOS device and iCloud backups) and may restore it if you reinstall. This is controlled by your device settings and by Google or Apple, not by the developer.

6. Third-party software

The App is built with the Flutter framework and open-source libraries. As used by the App they do not collect or transmit personal data. Their licence notices are on the Third-party notices page and inside the App under Settings > Open-source licenses.

7. App stores and purchases

The App is distributed through Apple's App Store and Google Play. Those companies process your account, download and payment information under their own privacy policies; the developer does not receive your payment details.

8. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Contacting the developer

If you e-mail the developer, the developer will receive your e-mail address and message and will use them only to reply.

10. Changes

If the App's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released.
